Lemon Law Representation in Houston, TX

A vehicle may stall at a Houston intersection, lose power on the freeway, or show a warning light after startup. The owner may have brought it back to the repair shop three times for the same problem.

The owner has usually requested another inspection and kept repair orders, warranty papers, invoices, and notes about each visit. We review that file and discuss its next stage with vehicle owners in Houston, West University Place, Southside Place, and Bellaire.

A transmission that slips again after a repair may call for closer review if the repair order identifies the same condition. A different notation or separate cause can change that review. We tell the owner which applies before anything is started.
