Lemon Law Matters in Houston, TX

We hear about vehicles that stall at intersections, lose power on freeways, or show warning lights after a repair visit. A defect can return on another repair order. The vehicle has been back to the service department more than once.

Owners often have repair orders, warranty papers, invoices, and text messages after returning the vehicle to the service department. We organize the file and compare reported defects with repair dates. We serve Houston, West University Place, Southside Place, and Bellaire.

We see recurring transmission shift problems lead to another repair attempt. We examine notes and whether the condition returns while driving. We compare complete records with missing dates; each can change the path. We tell owners which applies before starting.
