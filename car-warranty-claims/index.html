Car Warranty Claims in Houston, TX

We review warranty repairs, shop visits, and dealer records for Houston vehicle owners.

Call (346) 214-1590 Ask a question

People call us saying, “My car is still under warranty, but the dealer keeps sending me home,” or “The same light came back.” We translate those reports into a review of warranty coverage, repair history, and the vehicle defect.

Some owners describe a car that has been in the shop repeatedly. Others have one repair order that documents a serious problem. A repair order can tell a different story from the one told at the service counter.

This is worth looking at now because warranty dates, mileage, and shop visits must be matched to records. Locating a complete repair trail gets harder after invoices, emails, and dealer notes are scattered.

Warning lights and repeat repairs

Drivers describe a check engine light that returns after a repair. They report a transmission that hesitates or slips. Others describe a backup camera that goes black or air conditioning that blows warm.

We also hear about a vehicle that will not start after sitting overnight. A dashboard message may appear briefly, then disappear before the service appointment. Those reports can sound alike from the driveway, but we review each repair history and fault separately.

Case file review

We first identify the vehicle, reported fault, purchase or lease status, and repair sequence. We can usually establish the basic concern during the first conversation.

We then read the purchase or lease agreement, repair orders, and dates the vehicle entered and left the shop. We compare the written complaint with the technician notes, mileage, warranty terms, and dealer correspondence. If a document is missing, we determine where the record may be available before assessing the file further.

Repair attempts and warranty dates

We look at the number of repair attempts for the same fault and the days the vehicle was out of service. We also check whether each shop visit produced a repair order with the owner’s complaint, diagnosis, and work performed.

A repair order stating that a technician could not duplicate the issue may matter differently than an order showing a replacement part. We review whether the defect was reported while the warranty was active and whether the vehicle was bought or leased.

We also consider who holds the key records, including the dealer, repair facility, lender, or owner. No single warning light or shop visit answers every warranty question, so we weigh the full repair record against the warranty terms.

Repair orders and VIN

We use repair orders and the vehicle identification number to trace the service history. The VIN is commonly printed on the purchase or lease agreement, insurance card, registration, and driver-side dashboard.

We compare the in and out dates, mileage, complaint, diagnosis, and repair work on every shop invoice. We also review emails, text messages, and letters exchanged with the dealer about the defect.

When owners call Lemon Law Firm Houston at (346) 214-1590, we can begin with the records already available. Reconstructing exact service dates and locating complete dealer repair orders gets harder if this matter waits.
